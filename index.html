Coming soon to the App Store How it plays

Level 38, Pump House, on iPhone Duo.

A small snowy city is printed across the two halves of your iPhone Duo. Fold the phone and the right-hand page tips, so every stone on it rolls downhill.

Open flat and walk

Tap the ground to walk the streets while the page lies flat.

Fold to tip the page

Fold part-way for a gentle tip, further for a steep one. Stones roll until a cup, a gap or a wall stops them.

Light the lantern

Fill every cup and the lantern lights. Do it in the fewest folds for three stars.

The hinge is the controller

Tip the page. Roll the stones.

The order you fold in is the puzzle. Tip too early and a stone drops into the wrong cup; tip too steep and it jumps the one you wanted.

50 hand-made levels

Fill every cup. Light the lantern.

From a first gentle roll to The Last Lantern, with drawbridges, level crossings, bollards, snowdrifts and pressure plates, each introduced on its own level. Every level is checked by a solver, so there is always a way through.

Snow, dusk and midnight

A quiet city to get lost in.

Every street has its own hour, from bright midday after snowfall to blue hour and lamplit midnight, with a gentle soundtrack underneath.

Play your way

Hinge Assist puts an on-screen dial in place of folding, for anyone who would rather not fold.

Progress in your iCloud

Your stars follow you through your own iCloud. Only your Apple Account can read them.

No ads, no tracking

No accounts and no analytics. Creasora collects no data at all.

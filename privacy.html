Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

In short: Creasora does not collect any information about you. The only thing that leaves your phone is your level progress, which Apple stores in your own iCloud account so it can follow you to your other devices. We cannot see it.

Who we are

Creasora is a puzzle game for iPhone Duo made by Efe Havza (“we”, “us”). This policy covers the Creasora app and this website.

Information we collect

None. Creasora has no accounts of its own, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking, and it contains no third-party code that collects data. We never receive your name, contact details, location, device identifiers or anything about how you play.

Information stored on your iPhone

  • Game progress: your best result for each level (stars and number of folds) and which levels you have unlocked.
  • Settings: sound and music volume, haptics and Hinge Assist.
  • Small reminders, such as whether you have already seen a level’s introduction card.

This information stays on your device and is used only to run the game.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, your game progress (and only your game progress) is also saved in your iCloud account using Apple’s iCloud key-value storage, so you can continue on another device. This copy is stored by Apple under your Apple Account and is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy. We have no access to it. If you are not signed in to iCloud, your progress stays on your iPhone only.

Sensors

Creasora reads the angle of your iPhone Duo’s hinge and the direction of gravity from the motion sensors, so that folding the phone tips the city. These readings are used in the moment while you play. They are never saved or sent anywhere.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any information, because we do not have any. No third party receives data from Creasora.

Keeping and deleting your information

  • Deleting Creasora from your iPhone deletes everything the game stored on that device.
  • The progress saved in iCloud belongs to your Apple Account. It stays there if you reinstall the game, and it is removed with your iCloud data when you delete it through Apple, for example by deleting your Apple Account’s iCloud data.
  • To stop syncing at any time, sign out of iCloud on your device.

Children

Creasora is suitable for all ages. It collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

This website

This site is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics or tracking. Like any web host, GitHub may keep standard server logs, as described in GitHub’s Privacy Statement. The pages load a typeface from Google Fonts.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If Creasora ever starts handling information differently, the policy will be updated before that version of the app is released.

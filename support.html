Frequently asked questions

Which devices does Creasora work on?

iPhone Duo with iOS 27.1 or later. You play by folding the phone, so the game needs the iPhone Duo hinge.

Why do I see “Made for iPhone Duo”?

The game shows this screen on iPhones without a hinge, where it can’t be played. If you see it on an iPhone Duo, make sure it is updated to iOS 27.1 or later, then quit and reopen Creasora. If it still appears, please email us.

How do I play?

Open your iPhone Duo flat and tap Play. Tap the ground to walk. Fold the phone part-way and the right-hand page tips, so every stone on it rolls downhill until it drops into a cup or hits a wall. Fill every cup to light the lantern, then walk to it.

What are folds and stars?

Each level allows a limited number of folds, shown at the top of the screen. Finishing in the fewest possible folds earns three stars. If you run out, tap restart to try again. Every level has been checked by a solver, so there is always a way through.

Can I play without folding the phone?

Yes. Pause the game, open Settings and turn on Hinge Assist. An on-screen dial then tips the page for you.

Does my progress carry over to another device?

Yes. If you are signed in to iCloud, your stars and unlocked levels sync through your own iCloud account. If two devices disagree, Creasora keeps the better result for each level. Sync can take a few minutes.

There’s no sound.

Check that your iPhone isn’t in Silent mode, then turn up the volume. Music and effects each have their own slider in Settings inside the game.

Does Creasora collect my data?

No. There are no ads, accounts, analytics or tracking. See the Privacy Policy for details.

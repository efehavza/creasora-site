CREASORA

Fold the city. Roll the stones.

Made for iPhone Duo

Coming soon to the App Store.

A small snowy city is printed across the two halves of your iPhone Duo. Fold the phone and the right-hand page tips: every stone on it rolls downhill. Fill the cups, light the lantern, and do it in as few folds as you can.

You fold the phone

The hinge is the controller. Open flat to walk, fold part-way for a gentle tip, further for a steep one.

50 hand-made levels

From a first gentle roll to The Last Lantern, with drawbridges, level crossings, snowdrifts and pressure plates along the way.

Quiet and private

No ads, no accounts, no tracking. Your progress syncs through your own iCloud, and nothing else leaves your phone.

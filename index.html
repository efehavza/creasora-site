Coming soon to the App Store How it plays

Level 38, Pump House

A small snowy city is printed across the two halves of your iPhone Duo. Fold the phone and the right-hand page tips, so every stone on it rolls downhill. Fill the cups, light the lantern, and do it in as few folds as you can.

The hinge is the controller

Tip the page. Roll the stones.

Open flat to walk the streets. Fold part-way for a gentle tip, further for a steep one. Every stone rolls until it drops into a cup, a gap or a wall, so the order you fold in is the puzzle.

50 hand-made levels

Fill every cup. Light the lantern.

From a first gentle roll to The Last Lantern, with drawbridges, level crossings, bollards, snowdrifts and pressure plates, each introduced on its own level. Every level is checked by a solver, so there is always a way through.

Snow, dusk and midnight

A quiet city to get lost in.

Each street has its own hour, from bright midday after snowfall to blue hour and lamplit midnight, with a gentle soundtrack. Three stars wait for anyone who finds the fewest folds.

Play your way

Hinge Assist puts an on-screen dial in place of folding, for anyone who would rather not fold.

Progress in your iCloud

Your stars follow you through your own iCloud. Only your Apple Account can read them.

Nothing else

No ads, no accounts, no tracking. Creasora collects no data at all.
